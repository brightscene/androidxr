Lights · AudioStrobe & SpectraStrobe

Light-and-sound tracks hide light signals in the audio. MindLyte decodes them and flashes a symbol for each eye at the encoded rate and colour, like mind-machine glasses. Import an encoded track below.

Bring your own AI track

Load a track you generated elsewhere (Suno, Udio, MP3/WAV). MindLyte analyses its beats and dynamics to key the visuals, and layers the tones underneath.

In the headset: point and pinch to choose. Binaural tones need headphones; isochronic tones work on speakers.

Flashing lights warning

Lights mode flashes light at rates between about 1 and 40 times a second. Flashing light at these rates can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, sometimes for the first time.

  • Don't use it if you or anyone in your family has had epilepsy or seizures, or if you have a serious eye condition, a head injury or a heart condition, or you're pregnant.
  • Stop straight away if you feel dizzy, sick or disoriented, or notice twitching or changes in your vision.
  • Don't use it while driving or operating machinery, or after alcohol or other drugs.

You can lower the brightness or choose softened pulses at any time.
