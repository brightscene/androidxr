Stillpoint

Generative soundscapes tuned to the nine solfeggio frequencies, with spatial light that moves with the music.

Length
Bring your own AI track

Load a track you generated elsewhere (e.g. Suno or Udio, MP3/WAV). Stillpoint analyses its beats and dynamics to key the visuals, and layers the selected solfeggio tone underneath.

In the headset: point and pinch to choose. Binaural beats work best with headphones.
